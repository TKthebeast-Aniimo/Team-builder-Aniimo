BUILD • ANALYZE • COUNTER

Build a team around the Aniimo you actually want to use.

Choose any four Aniimo. There are no forced role slots. The builder looks at roles, stats, elements, traits, skills, buffs, debuffs, Break and sustain to explain how the team can work.

94 numbered Aniimo

1. Choose your four Aniimo

Duplicate role combinations are allowed. You can use four DPS, two Supports, three Break, etc.

2. Aniimo roster

Search, filter by role or element, then tap an Aniimo to add it to the next empty slot.

How the score works

This is an explainable heuristic, not an official in-game rating.

Synergy buffs, debuffs, Break windows, elemental interactions and role complementarity
Damage Attack, DPS presence and offensive skill evidence
Sustain Heal/Regen roles and healing/EP evidence
Weakness directional elemental matchups and over-reliance on one damage element

Unofficial fan project. Aniimo names, artwork and game information belong to their respective owners.

Factual data is intended to be sourced from the Official Aniimo Wiki . Aniidex is used as a secondary reference.

Data is refreshed by the included GitHub Action. Always re-check game information after official patches.
